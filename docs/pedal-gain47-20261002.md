## Behavior

All modes use post-blend linear gain of 1.7179084 (+4.7 dB) at patch Mix noon and above. Below noon, gain interpolates in amplitude from unity at effective mix0 to that value at effective mix0.5. The 0..5% true-dry zone remains bit-exact unity, avoiding a gain jump at its edge. This interpolation is a diagnostic implementation choice to preserve the dry endpoint, not an analog circuit claim. Mix remains a dry/wet control; full Mix is delayed wet-only audio, not a return to dry.

Tone, filters, Width, delays, rates and mode fades remain unchanged. Blue I+II remains fast common modulation; Ryan dislikes its fast vibrato character, which is recorded for a later explicit voicing decision. `ENDLESS_DESKTOP` builds bypass this built-in correction and retain their existing adjustable Output trim, preventing a second +4.7 dB boost in saved audition projects.
